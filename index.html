Privacy Policy — Salesforce Property Workspace

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Overview

Salesforce Property Workspace (formerly CoStar → Salesforce Property Sync) is an internal Rein & Grossoehme productivity tool for creating and updating Properties, Accounts, Contacts, and Occupancies in your authorized Salesforce organization.

Information used

  • Business and contact details you enter, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, mailing addresses, and property or tenancy information.
  • Existing Salesforce records and metadata, including record types and picklist values, to fill forms, check for duplicates, and show proposed changes.
  • The active page's URL and rendered content while the side panel or pop-out window is open. On CoStar this includes property details. On business websites this includes company names, phone numbers, email addresses, and business addresses. The extension also checks open Salesforce property tabs to suggest a matching record. It does not collect your browsing history, crawl websites, or navigate source pages automatically.
  • Salesforce authorization tokens and the address of your Salesforce organization. The extension does not receive your Salesforce password.

Local storage

Authorization tokens, Salesforce metadata, active-user lookup results, and CoStar-mode preferences are stored in Chrome's local extension storage. In-progress forms are kept in Chrome session storage. Session drafts are cleared when the browser session ends and when you finish or cancel the relevant form. Metadata and active-user results are cached to reduce repeated lookups. No information is sent to a server operated by the extension developer, and the extension does not use Chrome sync storage.

Information sent to Salesforce

The extension sends search criteria to your Salesforce organization to find records and check for duplicates. When you choose Create, approve an update, or use the CoStar Send without review action, the selected record fields are sent to your organization. Contact phone numbers are also saved as linked Phone Number records. Source websites are read locally; the extension makes no network requests to those websites.

Salesforce also receives authorization and token-renewal requests to sign you in and keep your authorized session active. Salesforce is the only external service the extension communicates with. Its handling of your organization's records is governed by your organization's Salesforce arrangements and Salesforce's privacy statement: https://www.salesforce.com/company/privacy/.

Data use and sharing

Information is used only to provide these Salesforce record-management features. The extension does not sell data, send data to advertising or analytics services, use it for unrelated purposes, or use it to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility. There is no advertising, analytics, or telemetry.

Permissions

Storage keeps authorization, preferences, metadata, and drafts locally. Identity enables Salesforce sign-in. Tabs and activeTab identify the current page and relevant open Salesforce records. Scripting and access to websites allow the extension to read the active page while its panel or pop-out is open; prospect businesses can use any website domain. SidePanel displays the workspace alongside your browser. The extension does not modify source web pages.

Retention and your controls

Disconnect attempts to revoke Salesforce authorization and removes locally stored tokens and cached Salesforce metadata and users. You can also revoke access through Salesforce's connected-app settings. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage. Clearing a local draft or uninstalling does not delete records already saved in Salesforce; those records follow your organization's retention and access rules.

Policy changes

This page will be updated when the extension's data handling changes, with the revision date shown above.
